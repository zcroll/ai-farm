## Getting Started

### Registration and Login

1. Visit the application at your domain (e.g., http://localhost:8000)
2. Click on "Register" to create a new account
3. Fill in your name, email, and password
4. Once registered, you'll be automatically logged in and redirected to the dashboard
5. For future visits, use the "Login" button with your email and password

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is your central hub for plant disease detection and provides:

- **Statistics Cards**: Shows the total number of scans you've performed, how many plants were healthy, and how many had diseases
- **Scanning Section**: Upload or capture plant leaf images for analysis
- **Recent History**: View your most recent scan results

## Scanning Plants

You can scan plants in two ways:

### Method 1: Upload an Image

1. On the dashboard, click the file input field
2. Select an image of a plant leaf from your device
3. Click the "Analyze Plant" button
4. Wait for the analysis to complete (usually takes a few seconds)

### Method 2: Use Your Camera

1. On the dashboard, click the "Use Camera" button
2. Grant permission to access your camera if prompted
3. Position the plant leaf in the frame
4. Click the "Take Photo" button
5. Click the "Analyze Plant" button
6. Wait for the analysis to complete

### Best Practices for Scanning

- Use well-lit images with good focus
- Capture a single leaf against a plain background when possible
- Ensure the diseased area is clearly visible
- Avoid extreme angles or shadows
- For best results, take close-up images of the affected area

## Understanding Results

After scanning, you'll see the analysis results:

- **Disease Name**: The identified plant disease or "healthy" status
- **Confidence Score**: How confident the AI is in its diagnosis (percentage)
- **Description**: Information about the identified disease
- **Treatment Suggestions**: Recommended actions to treat the disease
- **Source Link**: Additional resources for more information (if available)

### Confidence Score

- **90-100%**: Very high confidence in the diagnosis
- **70-89%**: High confidence in the diagnosis
- **50-69%**: Moderate confidence, consider a second scan or consult an expert
- **Below 50%**: Low confidence, try rescanning with a clearer image

## Viewing Scan History

To view your complete scan history:

1. Click on "Scan History" in the sidebar navigation
2. Browse through all your previous scans
3. Use pagination controls to navigate through multiple pages
4. Click "View Details" on any scan to see the full analysis

### Scan Detail Page

The scan detail page provides:

- A larger view of the scanned image
- Complete disease information
- Treatment recommendations
- Date and time of the scan
- Option to return to history or scan another plant

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Image Not Uploading**
- Ensure the image is in a supported format (JPG, JPEG, PNG)
- Check that the file size is under 2MB
- Try a different browser or device

**Camera Not Working**
- Grant camera permissions in your browser settings
- Ensure no other applications are using your camera
- Try refreshing the page

**Inaccurate Results**
- Try taking a clearer, better-lit photo
- Ensure the diseased area is prominently visible
- Take multiple scans from different angles

**Loading Takes Too Long**
- Check your internet connection
- Refresh the page and try again
- Reduce the image size before uploading
